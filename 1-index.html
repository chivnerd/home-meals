Teriyaki Beef + Broccoli Noodles

Serves 5 · ~30 min
gluten-freedairy-freenightshade-free
✓ Corey✓ Wife✓ Girl 1✓ Girl 2✓ Son
~650cal
44gprotein
68gcarbs
18gfat

Ingredients

  • 2 lb ground sirloin
  • 1 box Jovial GF brown-rice spaghetti
  • 1 broccoli crown, cut into florets
  • 1 yellow onion, sliced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • Thumb of ginger, grated
  • 4 tbsp tamari
  • 3 tbsp coconut aminos
  • 3 tbsp maple syrup
  • Black pepper, white pepper, and salt
  • Star anise or a cinnamon stick (optional)
  • Toasted sesame oil (optional)
  • Sesame seeds (optional)
  • Scallions, sliced (optional)

Steps

  1. Salt the pasta water and cook the GF spaghetti just shy of done. Reserve a cup of pasta water before draining.
  2. Drop the broccoli florets into the pasta water for the last 2-3 minutes, or steam them separately.
  3. High heat, a little oil. Brown the beef with black pepper, white pepper, and salt, breaking it up well. Drain excess fat.
  4. Push the beef aside, drop to medium heat. Onion in, 3-4 minutes, then garlic and grated ginger for 1 minute.
  5. Add the sauce: tamari, coconut aminos, and maple syrup (simmer in the star anise or cinnamon stick if using). Scrape the pan and let it bubble 1-2 minutes.
  6. Toss in the pasta and broccoli. Loosen with pasta water as needed and adjust salt and sweetness to taste.
  7. Finish with toasted sesame oil, sesame seeds, and scallions. No chili flakes in the pan (nightshade allergy) - heat goes on at the table.
Wife-safe: celiac gluten-free, dairy-free, and nightshade-free.
